I am delighted to announce our paper [ReCodeAgent: A Multi-Agent Workflow for Language-agnostic Translation and Validation of Large-scale Repositories](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07341) has been accepted to ASE 2026. This work is a collaboration between UIUC [Intelligent CAT Lab](https://reyhaneh.cs.illinois.edu/lab.htm) and AWS. Looking forward to presenting it in Munich, Germany 🇩🇪
